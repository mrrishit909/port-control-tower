Port Control Tower

An invented container terminal at 10:00. One ship is hours late and lands on another's berth; the yard has to be stacked so nobody digs; 1,610 trucks are coming to a gate that serves 96 an hour. …
The terminal, its vessels, containers, AIS and trucks are generated by a simulator in this repository; nothing is a real port. Crane rates, lane times and the yard geometry are stated assumptions. No outside model or service is called.
